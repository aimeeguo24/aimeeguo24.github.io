About

Hi, I'm Aimee Guo.

Biomedical engineering & CS student at Cornell, building AI-driven tools for cancer diagnostics and healthcare accessibility.

Curious, empathetic, imaginative — I'm a freshman at Cornell University from Dallas, Texas, focused on creating a positive impact in my community through biomedical engineering, computer science, and music. My work sits at the intersection of AI and medicine, from fine-tuning segmentation models for cancer imaging to building tools that make healthcare more accessible. Outside of research, I lead app-development teams and community initiatives, and I'm a classically trained pianist.

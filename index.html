About

Curious, empathetic, imaginative — I'm a freshman at Cornell University from Dallas, Texas, focused on creating a positive impact in my community through biomedical engineering, computer science, and music. My work sits at the intersection of AI and medicine, from fine-tuning segmentation models for cancer imaging to building tools that make healthcare more accessible. Outside of research, I lead app-development teams and community initiatives, and I'm a classically trained pianist.

Research

Regeneron Science Talent Search (STS) Scholar, 2025

ClickSAM

Fine-tuned the Segment Anything Model with self-generated click prompts for ultrasound image segmentation. Published at SPIE Medical Imaging 2024 (first author).

Segmentation, ultrasound imaging, deep learning

Marker Track

Automated fiducial marker tracking to assess residual motion during breath-hold radiation therapy. Submitted to Biomedical Physics & Engineering Express (first author).

Computer vision, radiation therapy

AR Tracheal Intubation

Augmented reality system guiding physicians through tracheal intubation procedures.

DOI: 10.1117/12.3007671

Hand Gesture Recognition for Home Automation

Built a computer-vision pipeline for hand gesture classification to assist elderly and disabled individuals with daily tasks. UT Dallas Intelligent Robotics & Vision Lab, Dr. Xiang (Summer 2022).

GitHub

Ultrasound-Guided 3D Prostate Cancer Detection

Converted 2D ultrasound segments into 3D models using ImageJ and 3D Slicer to help identify prostate cancer. UT Dallas Quantitative Bioimaging Lab, Dr. Fei (Summer 2023).
